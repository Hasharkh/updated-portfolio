How I work

I'm Hashar Ahmed. Most of my work starts with a design someone else made, in Figma or Lovable, and a site that needs to exist by a certain date. My job is to build it so it holds up: on every screen size, in the hands of the people who will edit it, and months after launch.

I don't follow a design blindly. If a section would read better with different spacing, or a layout breaks on mobile, I say so and show an alternative. When a project needs something a page builder can't do, like syncing jobs from an external system, I write the custom plugin myself.

I ask questions early, keep you updated, and stay patient through revisions until everything works as expected.
